Common error

"This Phone Number Cannot Be Used for Verification": Why It Happens

Google shows this message when a number has already been used too many times, or when its signals look unreliable. Public receive-SMS numbers trigger it almost every time, because hundreds of strangers have used them before you.

Retrying the same number rarely helps. Use a private number such as Google Voice or TextNow, or a private paid number such as SMSFAST if you are outside the US. If Google offers to skip the phone step, take it and add a recovery email instead.

Options

Can You Create a Google Account Without a Phone Number?

Sometimes. Google decides whether to ask for a phone number based on the device, the network and other risk signals. On a phone you already use, the phone step is often optional. On a new browser or a shared network, Google is more likely to require it. There is no setting that guarantees you can skip it, and we do not recommend tricks to avoid it.

Free US number

Google Voice: Free US Number, With a Catch

Google Voice gives you a free US number for calls and texts, linked to your Google account. It is the most stable free number we test: it has no ads and is not recycled as quickly as ad-supported apps, as long as you use it.

The catch is setup. To claim a Google Voice number you need an existing US phone number to verify, and the service is meant for personal use in the US. If you are in the UK, Canada or Australia without a US number, TextNow or a paid number are the realistic options. See our free US virtual number page for a full comparison.

Step by step

How to Verify a Google Account With a Virtual Number

  1. Get a private number

    Set up Google Voice or TextNow, or buy a paid number if you are outside the US.

  2. Enter it with the country code

    Use the +1 format for US and Canadian numbers.

  3. Choose SMS or voice call

    Google can send a text or call with the code.

  4. Enter the code quickly

    Codes expire after a few minutes.

  5. Add a recovery email

    Do not rely on the virtual number alone to recover the account later.

Security

Keep Your Google Account Safe

Never set a public number as your Google recovery phone: whoever has the number can reset your password. After signing up, add a recovery email, turn on 2-Step Verification with an authenticator app or a passkey, and remove any virtual number you might lose.

FAQ

Free Virtual Number for Google Verification: FAQ

Which free virtual numbers work for Google verification?

In our tests Google Voice passed every time and TextNow passed 9 of 10 attempts. Talkatone and TextFree passed some attempts. Public receive-SMS numbers almost always failed.

Why does Google say this phone number cannot be used for verification?

The number has been used too many times or looks unreliable to Google. It happens most with public receive-SMS numbers. Try a private number instead.

Can I create a Google account without a phone number?

Sometimes. Google decides based on your device and other signals. On a familiar device the phone step is often optional.

How do I get a free Google Voice number?

Sign in at voice.google.com with your Google account, choose a number and verify it with an existing US phone number. It is available for personal use in the US.

Should I use a virtual number as my Google recovery phone?

Only if it is a private number you will keep. Never use a public number, and add a recovery email and an authenticator app as well.
