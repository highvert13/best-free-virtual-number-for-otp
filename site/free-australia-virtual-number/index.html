Number format

Australian Phone Number Format

Australian numbers use the country code +61. Mobile numbers start with 4 after the country code, for example +61 412 345 678. Locally the same number is written 0412 345 678: drop the leading 0 when you add +61.

What exists

Free Australian Numbers: Public Sites, Trials and Shared Numbers

Free +61 numbers come in three forms. Public receive-SMS websites such as Quackr and SMS24 show every message to everyone. Business phone providers sometimes offer short free trials. A few services offer shared numbers that many customers use at once.

None of these suits an account you want to keep. Public and shared numbers are quickly blocked by Telegram, WhatsApp and Google, and trials end. For a long-term Australian account, use your own mobile number or a private number rented from a provider such as SMSFAST.

Do you need one?

Do You Need a Local Australian Number?

Telegram and Google accept foreign numbers, so a free US number can verify an account opened in Australia. Australian banks and WhatsApp are more likely to want a local +61 mobile number.

FAQ

Free Australian Virtual Number: FAQ

Can I get a free Australian number for OTP?

Only public ones. Quackr and SMS24 list free +61 numbers, but every code is public and most numbers are blocked by major apps.

Do free +61 numbers work for Telegram?

Sometimes. Quackr's Australian numbers passed some Telegram checks in our test. SMS24 failed.

What is the cheapest Australian number for verification?

Pay-per-code services are the cheapest. SMSFAST Australian numbers start from $0.21 per code and passed all three platforms in our test.

Can I use a foreign number for Australian apps?

Telegram and Google accept foreign numbers. Australian banks, government services and WhatsApp may require a local +61 number.
