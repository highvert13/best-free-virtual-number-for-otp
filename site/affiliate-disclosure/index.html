Summary

OTP Number Lab links to SMSFAST, a paid virtual number service. We may receive compensation from SMSFAST when visitors use it. Other services in our ranking are mentioned by name only, without links, and we receive nothing from them.

How this affects the ranking

  • Free services are ranked first and are ordered only by our test results.
  • SMSFAST is scored with exactly the same monthly tests as every other service.
  • We would remove or move down SMSFAST if it stopped passing our tests.

How links are marked

Links to SMSFAST are shown as buttons labelled "Get number" or as text links naming SMSFAST, and open in a new tab. No other external service is linked from our ranking.

Disclosure rules

We follow the disclosure principles of the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and the UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) and Advertising Standards Authority (ASA): relationships that could affect how you read our content are stated clearly.
