Number format

Canadian Phone Number Format

Canadian numbers use the country code +1, the same as the US, followed by a 3-digit area code and a 7-digit number, for example +1 416 555 0142 (Toronto) or +1 604 555 0142 (Vancouver). From the number alone, apps cannot tell a Canadian number from a US one.

Good to know

Canada Shares +1 With the US: What That Means

Because Canada and the US share the +1 country code, most apps treat their numbers the same way. A free US number from TextNow or Talkatone can verify Telegram or Google for an account opened in Canada, and a Canadian number works for US services that only check the country code.

It matters when a service checks the area code. Canadian banks, government services and some delivery apps may require a Canadian area code, and will usually refuse VoIP lines anyway. For those, use your own mobile number. For everything else, compare options on our free US virtual number page as well.

Do you need one?

Do You Need a Local Canadian Number?

For Telegram and Google, a Canadian or US number is equally fine. For WhatsApp and local Canadian services, a number with a Canadian area code from a provider that passes carrier checks is the safer choice.
