About OTP Number Lab

Monthly, hands-on checks of free virtual numbers for verification codes.

Our mission

Lists of "free virtual numbers" go out of date fast: a number that worked last month may be blocked today. OTP Number Lab answers one question: which free numbers actually receive verification codes right now? We check them every month and publish the results.

What we cover

  • Platforms: Telegram, WhatsApp and Google account verification.
  • Countries: the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.
  • Number types: free app numbers, public receive-SMS websites and online number services.

Editorial policy

  • Checked, not copied. Every result on the site comes from our own checks.
  • Updated monthly. Services that stop working move down or leave the ranking.
  • Corrections welcome. If something is wrong, tell us and we fix it, noting the change in the changelog.
  • No bypass guides. We do not publish ways to get around platform security checks.
