Method

How We Count

  • Source: the public Apple App Store review feed for each app, in the US, Canadian, UK and Australian stores. Up to 150 of the newest reviews per store are read every day.
  • Window: only reviews from the last 30 days are counted.
  • Code-related: the title or text mentions codes, verification, OTP, 2FA, sign-up, log-in, WhatsApp, Telegram or a Google account.
  • Quotes: the newest negative and positive code-related review, shortened. Reviewer names, reviews with personal details and reviews written by children are never shown.
  • Which services: only services in our ranking that have an iPhone app in the App Store. Web-based services and public receive-SMS websites have no App Store reviews, so they are not listed here.
  • Limits: App Store reviews are a small, self-selected sample and do not prove that an app works or fails for you. Google Play reviews are not included.
