Method

How We Count

  • Source: the public Apple App Store review feed for each app, in the US, Canadian, UK and Australian stores. Up to 150 of the newest reviews per store are read every day. If the feed is temporarily empty, the previous day's figures are kept.
  • Window: only reviews from the last 30 days are counted.
  • Code-related: the title or text mentions codes, verification, OTP, 2FA, sign-up, log-in, WhatsApp, Telegram or a Google account.
  • Quotes: the newest negative and positive code-related review, shortened. Reviewer names, reviews with personal details and reviews written by children are never shown.
  • Which services: services in our ranking that have an iPhone app in the App Store. Web-based services have no App Store reviews; where a service publishes buyer reviews on its own website, we show those separately and never mix them into the App Store figures. Public receive-SMS websites have no reviews to read.
  • Critical: for App Store reviews, 1–2 stars; for reviews on a service's own site, the reviews that site marks as negative or that give 1–2 stars.
  • Limits: App Store reviews are a small, self-selected sample and do not prove that an app works or fails for you. Google Play reviews are not included.
