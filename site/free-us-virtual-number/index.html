Number format

US Phone Number Format

US numbers use the country code +1 followed by a 10-digit number: a 3-digit area code and a 7-digit local number, for example +1 415 555 0142. Mobile and landline numbers look the same, so apps check the line type separately. Enter the number with +1 and no extra zero.

How to keep a free US number active

Free numbers go back to the pool when you stop using them, and the next owner can receive your codes. Send a text or make a call regularly: Talkatone, for example, expects activity at least every 30 days. Check each app's current inactivity rules, as they change from time to time.

Do you need one?

Do You Need a Local US Number?

For Telegram and Google, any working number is fine, so a US number can verify an account opened anywhere. Local US services such as banks, delivery apps and some marketplaces may require a US number and will usually reject VoIP lines. WhatsApp may also expect a number that matches your region.

FAQ

Free US Virtual Number: FAQ

Where can I get a free US number for OTP?

TextNow, Google Voice, Talkatone, TextFree and Nextplus all offer free US numbers. In our tests TextNow and Google Voice had the best OTP results.

Is Google Voice free outside the US?

Google Voice is meant for personal use in the US and needs an existing US number to set up. Outside the US, TextNow or a paid number are easier options.

Can I get a free US number without a US phone?

Yes. TextNow and Talkatone sign up with an email address. Google Voice is the one that requires an existing US number.

Do free US numbers work for WhatsApp?

Rarely. Only Google Voice passed some WhatsApp checks in our test. For WhatsApp, a paid number is the realistic choice.

How long does a free US number last?

As long as you keep using it. Most free apps reclaim numbers after a period of inactivity, so send a text or make a call regularly.
