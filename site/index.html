Step by step

How to Receive an OTP With a Free Virtual Number

Receiving a verification code without a SIM card takes about two minutes. Follow these five steps.

  1. Pick the right number type

    Use a private app such as TextNow for accounts you keep. Use a public receive-SMS site only for throwaway signups.

  2. Check the number is fresh

    On public sites, pick a number that received messages in the last hour. Old numbers are usually blocked by Telegram, WhatsApp and Google.

  3. Enter the number with the country code

    Use +1 for the US and Canada, +44 for the UK and +61 for Australia. Choose a country that matches the app's region if it asks.

  4. Wait and refresh

    Codes usually arrive within a minute and expire after a few minutes. Refresh the inbox page or the app.

  5. Use a backup if no code arrives

    Request a voice-call code, wait before resending, or switch to a different number. Requesting codes again and again only triggers a cooldown.

Troubleshooting

Why Your OTP Code Isn't Arriving

The line is VoIP

Before sending a code, many platforms look up the number's carrier and line type. If the lookup says VoIP, the request is rejected silently and no SMS is sent.

The number is overused

Public numbers receive hundreds of codes a day from strangers. Platforms notice this pattern and block the number for everyone.

You hit the resend limit

Repeated requests trigger a cooldown that can last from minutes to a day. Wait before trying again, or ask for a voice call.

Almost every free virtual number is a VoIP number: it runs over the internet and is not tied to a mobile carrier SIM. That is why free numbers fail more often than paid non-VoIP numbers, which come from real carriers and pass carrier checks. If you still get no code, try a non-VoIP number on SMSFAST or see our cheap paid alternatives.

Safety

Is It Safe to Use a Free Virtual Number for OTP?

Yes, for privacy and one-off signups. No, for accounts that hold money or personal data. Using a virtual number for your own privacy is legal in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia; mass account creation breaks platform rules.

Use it for

  • Keeping your real number private
  • Trial and one-time signups
  • Testing your own apps
  • A second Telegram account

Do not use it for

  • Banking and payment apps
  • Your primary email
  • Two-factor recovery
  • Accounts you cannot afford to lose

The biggest risk is losing the number. A free app can reclaim it after inactivity, and a public number can be used by anyone to reset your password. For two-factor authentication, an authenticator app is safer than any SMS number.

FAQ

Free Virtual Number for OTP: FAQ

What is the best free virtual number for OTP?

In our November 2026 tests, TextNow was the best free virtual number for OTP overall. It received Google codes in 9 of 10 attempts and Telegram codes in 6 of 10. It is available in the US and Canada. For WhatsApp, no free option was reliable, so we recommend a cheap paid number such as SMSFAST.

Can I use a free virtual number for Telegram?

Sometimes. Telegram accepts some free app numbers such as TextNow and Google Voice, but it rejects numbers it identifies as VoIP or overused and may show "phone number banned". Public receive-SMS numbers rarely work and are risky because anyone can read the code.

Is there a free number that works for WhatsApp verification?

Rarely. WhatsApp is the strictest of the three platforms we test. Most free VoIP numbers fail, and a public number can let another person re-register your account later. A cheap private paid number is the safer choice.

Why does Google say this phone number cannot be used for verification?

Google shows this message when a number has been used too many times or looks unreliable. Public receive-SMS numbers trigger it most often. A private number such as Google Voice or TextNow usually works better, and Google sometimes lets you skip the phone step.

Are free online SMS numbers private?

No. Public receive-SMS websites show every incoming message to all visitors, including your OTP. Free app numbers like TextNow are private to your account, but they require signup and regular use to keep the number.

Why is my virtual number not receiving OTP?

The most common reasons are that the platform detected a VoIP line, the number is overused, or you hit the resend limit. Try a voice-call code, wait a few minutes before requesting again, or switch to a different number type.

Is it legal to use a virtual number for verification?

Using a virtual number to protect your own privacy is legal in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. Creating accounts in bulk or impersonating someone else breaks platform terms and can be illegal.

Do free virtual numbers expire?

Yes. Free app numbers are usually reclaimed after a period of inactivity, and public numbers are rotated often. If you lose the number, you may lose access to any account tied to it, so do not use one for accounts you need to recover.
