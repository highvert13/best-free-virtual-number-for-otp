Telegram "This Phone Number Is Banned": What It Means and What to Do

Short answer

The message means Telegram has blocked that phone number, usually because it is a VoIP or recycled number, was used for many accounts, or was reported for spam. If the number is genuinely yours, contact Telegram support. If it is a shared or virtual number, use a different number.

Why it happens

Common Causes and What Each One Looks Like

CauseWhat you seeWhat to do
The number is a VoIP or flagged rangeBanned right at signup, before any code is sentUse a different number type.
The number was used for spam beforeA recycled number is banned on first tryAsk your provider for a new number.
Many accounts on one numberPublic receive-SMS numbers are banned for everyoneNever use public numbers for Telegram.
Your own account broke the rulesThe ban follows a period of messagingAppeal through Telegram support.

Step by step

How to Fix It

  1. Check if the number is yours

    If it is your own SIM number, the ban is about your account, not the number type.

  2. Contact Telegram support

    Use the support link shown in the app, or email Telegram's abuse address, and explain the case briefly.

  3. Do not create new accounts in a loop

    Each new attempt on a flagged number makes recovery less likely.

  4. Use a different number

    For a new account, pick a number that passed Telegram in our ranking.

  5. Protect the new account

    Turn on Two-Step Verification (a cloud password) right after signing in.

Avoid

What Not to Do

  • Do not try to get around the ban with scripts or modified apps: it breaks Telegram's terms.
  • Do not buy numbers from people selling "unbannable" accounts.
  • Do not use a public number you found on a website.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Is a Telegram number ban permanent?

Not always. Bans on your own number can sometimes be lifted after contacting support. Bans on shared or VoIP numbers usually stay.

Why is a brand-new virtual number already banned?

Virtual numbers are often recycled. Someone may have used it for spam before you got it.

Can I use Telegram without a phone number?

No. Telegram requires a phone number at signup.
