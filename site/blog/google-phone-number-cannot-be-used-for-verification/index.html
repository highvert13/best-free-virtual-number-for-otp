Google: "This Phone Number Cannot Be Used for Verification" — How to Fix It

Short answer

Google shows this error when the number has been used too many times or looks unreliable, which is almost always the case with public receive-SMS numbers. Use a private number that has not been used for Google before, try from a device you normally use, or skip the phone step if Google offers that option.

Why it happens

Common Causes and What Each One Looks Like

CauseWhat you seeWhat to do
Public or shared numberError appears immediatelyUse a private number.
Number used for many Google accountsWorked before, now refusedUse a number not linked to other Google accounts.
New device or networkGoogle insists on a phone and refuses several numbersTry from a device and network you normally use.
Too many attemptsError appears for every number you tryWait a few hours before trying again.

Step by step

How to Fix It

  1. Stop retrying the same number

    Google remembers refused numbers. The same number will keep failing.

  2. Use a private number

    A number only you use, such as a free app number, passes far more often than a public one.

  3. Try from a familiar device

    Google asks for a phone more often on new browsers and shared networks.

  4. Look for the skip option

    If Google lets you skip the phone step, skip it and add a recovery email instead.

  5. Compare numbers that passed

    See which services passed Google in our ranking.

Avoid

What Not to Do

  • Do not set a virtual number you might lose as your Google recovery phone.
  • Do not use tools that promise to bypass Google's phone check.
  • Do not try dozens of public numbers in a row.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Why does Google say this phone number cannot be used for verification?

The number has been used too many times or looks unreliable to Google. Public receive-SMS numbers trigger it almost every time.

Which numbers does Google accept?

Private numbers with little history pass most often. In our tests Google Voice and TextNow were accepted in most attempts.

Can I create a Google account without a phone number?

Sometimes. On a device you already use, Google often lets you skip the phone step.
