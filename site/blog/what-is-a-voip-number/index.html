What Is a VoIP Number, and Why Do So Many Apps Block It?

Short answer

A VoIP number is a phone number that works over the internet rather than through a mobile carrier's SIM. Apps can look up a number's line type before sending a code, and many refuse VoIP numbers because they are cheap and quick to get in bulk, which makes them popular for fake accounts.

How it works

What a VoIP number is

VoIP stands for Voice over Internet Protocol. A VoIP number is assigned by an internet calling provider instead of a mobile network. Calls and texts reach you through an app or a website, not through a SIM card in your phone.

Free number apps such as TextNow, Google Voice, Talkatone and TextFree all give you VoIP numbers. So do many business phone systems. The number looks like any other: a US VoIP number still starts with +1 and has ten digits.

How it works

How apps can tell

Every phone number belongs to a carrier, and that carrier is recorded in industry databases. Verification providers offer a line type lookup: send them a number, and they answer with the carrier name and whether the line is mobile, landline, fixed VoIP or non-fixed VoIP.

Many apps run this lookup the moment you type your number, before any code is sent. If the answer is "non-fixed VoIP", the app may refuse the number with a vague message such as "This number is not supported", or simply never send the code.

How it works

Why apps block them

Non-fixed VoIP numbers are free or very cheap and can be created in large numbers in a few minutes. That makes them a favourite tool for spam accounts, fake reviews and bonus abuse. For an app, blocking them removes a large share of fake signups at the cost of turning away some honest users.

Each platform draws the line differently. Messaging apps that tie an account to a phone number, such as WhatsApp, tend to be the strictest. Other services accept VoIP numbers but watch for numbers that have been used for many accounts.

How it works

What it means for you

  • If a code never arrives, the number type is the most likely reason, not your phone.
  • Retrying the same number rarely helps: the lookup gives the same answer every time.
  • A voice-call option sometimes works where SMS does not, but not if the number was refused outright.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Is a Google Voice number a VoIP number?

Yes. Google Voice numbers are internet numbers, and line type lookups usually report them as VoIP.

Can I tell if my number is VoIP?

Not from the digits alone. A line type lookup shows it, and the app that gave you the number usually says whether it is an internet number.

Is it illegal to use a VoIP number for verification?

No. Using a VoIP number for your own accounts is legal. Apps are simply free to refuse them.
