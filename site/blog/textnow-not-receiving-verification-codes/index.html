TextNow Not Receiving Verification Codes: Why It Happens and How to Fix It

Short answer

Most of the time TextNow is not broken: the app or website sending the code refuses VoIP numbers, or sends the code from a short code that TextNow cannot receive. Check the number format, ask for a voice call, wait out the resend timer, and if the service still refuses, use a different number for that one service.

Why it happens

Step by step

How to Fix It

  1. Check the number

    In TextNow, open the menu and copy your number. Enter it as +1 followed by 10 digits.

  2. Open the app and wait

    Keep TextNow open for two minutes after requesting the code; some codes arrive with a delay.

  3. Ask for a voice call

    If the service offers "Call me instead", choose it. TextNow receives calls from numbers that cannot text it.

  4. Wait before resending

    If nothing arrives, wait an hour. Repeated requests make the sender block the number for longer.

  5. Try another number for this service

    Some services never accept VoIP numbers. Pick another option from our ranking for that account.

Avoid

What Not to Do

  • Do not request codes again and again: it extends the block.
  • Do not use public receive-SMS numbers for an account you want to keep.
  • Do not rely on a TextNow number for banking or account recovery.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Why does TextNow receive normal texts but not verification codes?

Normal texts come from regular phone numbers. Many verification codes come from short codes or from systems that check the line type first, and they skip VoIP numbers such as TextNow.

Does TextNow work for Google verification?

Usually yes. In our tests Google accepted TextNow numbers in most attempts. Telegram accepted them about half the time, and WhatsApp rejected them.

Can I get a new TextNow number if mine is blocked?

Yes. You can change your number in the app settings. A new number has no history, so it is sometimes accepted where the old one was not.
