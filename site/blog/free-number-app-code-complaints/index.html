Which Free Number App Gets the Most Complaints About Codes? (November 2026)

Short answer

In the last 30 days, TextNow had the most App Store reviews about verification codes: 22 of 170 recent reviews, and 91% of those were 1–2 stars. More reviews also means more users, so compare the share of reviews, not only the count.

What it means

What This Means If You Need a Code

  • Recent ratings are lower than all-time ratings for every app. People with a problem are more likely to write a review, so use these numbers to compare apps, not to judge one app in isolation.
  • A spike in code complaints often means a platform started refusing that app's numbers. If your codes stop arriving, check our fixes before you request codes again.
  • Pricing complaints usually mean features moved behind a subscription, not that the free number stopped working.

Method

How We Counted

We read up to 150 of the newest App Store reviews per app in each of the US, Canadian, UK and Australian stores every day, and count reviews from the last 30 days. Topics are found by keywords. App Store reviews are a small, self-selected sample, and Google Play reviews are not included. Full details and recent quotes are on our user reports page.
