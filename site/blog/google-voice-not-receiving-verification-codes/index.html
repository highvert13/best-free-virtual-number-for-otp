Google Voice Not Receiving Verification Codes: Causes and Fixes

Short answer

Google Voice is a VoIP number, and many services either refuse VoIP numbers or send codes from short codes that Google Voice does not receive. Make sure messages are going to the Voice app, try a voice call, and if a service keeps refusing, verify that account with a different number.

Why it happens

Common Causes and What Each One Looks Like

CauseWhat you seeWhat to do
Short-code messagesCodes from banks, retailers or social apps never arriveAsk for a voice call or email code instead.
The service blocks VoIPThe site says the number is not supported, or no code is sentUse a different number for that service.
Messages forwarded elsewhereCodes go to email or a linked phone, not the Voice appIn Voice settings, check message forwarding.
Account or number on holdVoice shows a warning about your numberOpen voice.google.com and resolve the warning.
Too many attempts"Try again later" from the sending serviceWait at least an hour before trying again.

Step by step

How to Fix It

  1. Open Voice on the web

    Go to voice.google.com and check Messages. Codes sometimes appear there before the app syncs.

  2. Check forwarding settings

    In Settings, see where messages are forwarded and turn on notifications for the app.

  3. Request a voice call

    Choose "Call me" if the service offers it. Google Voice receives calls reliably.

  4. Wait before resending

    Give the sender at least an hour before you ask for another code.

  5. Use another number for that account

    If a service refuses VoIP, compare other options in our ranking.

Avoid

What Not to Do

  • Do not use Google Voice as the recovery number for your main Google account.
  • Do not let the number go inactive: Google can reclaim unused numbers.
  • Do not keep retrying the same service every few minutes.

FAQ

Questions People Ask

Can Google Voice receive short-code texts?

Often not. Many short-code senders do not deliver to Google Voice, which is why codes from banks and some apps never arrive.

Does WhatsApp accept Google Voice numbers?

Sometimes. In our tests WhatsApp accepted Google Voice in some attempts and refused it in others.

Why did my Google Voice number stop receiving codes?

The sending service may have started blocking VoIP numbers, or your Voice number may have a warning on the account. Check voice.google.com first.
