3. How we use your data and legal bases

  • To reply to your message, based on your consent and our legitimate interest in answering questions and correcting the ranking.
  • To understand how the site is used (analytics), based on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and working (server logs), based on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and fixing errors.
  • To meet legal obligations, when the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. Third-party services

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Hosting and security: Cloudflare, which delivers the site and processes server logs.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4, only with your consent, with IP anonymisation enabled.
  • Contact form processing: [provider to be confirmed], which receives the content of your message so it reaches us.

Our pages mention third-party services and may link to them. When you click such a link, you leave our site and that service's own privacy policy applies.

6. We do not provide phone numbers or receive your codes

OTP Number Lab only tests and reviews services. We do not issue phone numbers, we do not receive SMS on your behalf, and we cannot see the verification codes you receive on any service listed on this site. Any number you get comes from that service, under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. How long we keep data

  • Contact messages: up to 24 months after our last reply, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, the shortest retention available in Google Analytics 4.
  • Server logs: according to our hosting provider's retention, usually a few days.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw consent for analytics. We reply within 30 days. Specific rights depend on where you live:

United Kingdom (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

United States (including California, CCPA/CPRA)

California residents can request to know, delete and correct personal information, and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

Canada (PIPEDA)

You can access your personal information, ask for corrections and withdraw consent. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Australia (Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles)

You can access and correct your personal information. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

To make a request, contact us and choose "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. When we transfer data from the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent contractual protections.

10. Children

This site is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, show a notice on the site.
