Number format

UK Phone Number Format

UK numbers use the country code +44. Mobile numbers start with 7 after the country code, for example +44 7700 900142. In the UK the same number is written 07700 900142: drop the leading 0 when you add +44.

The honest answer

Why Free Private UK Numbers Are Rare

The free number apps that work well in North America, such as TextNow and Google Voice, do not offer +44 numbers. In the UK, free numbers come almost only from public receive-SMS websites, where every code is visible to all visitors and numbers are blocked quickly.

That leaves two realistic options. For a throwaway signup, try a fresh public number on Quackr. For an account you want to keep, or for WhatsApp, use a cheap private UK number from a provider such as SMSFAST. It costs less than a coffee and is not shared with strangers.

Do you need one?

Do You Need a Local UK Number?

Telegram and Google accept foreign numbers, so a free US number from TextNow can verify an account opened in the UK. UK banks, delivery apps and WhatsApp are more likely to want a local +44 number.

FAQ

Free UK Virtual Number: FAQ

How do I get a free UK phone number to receive SMS?

Public receive-SMS websites such as Quackr, Receive-SMSS and SMS24 list free +44 numbers. They work only for low-risk signups because every message is public.

Are free UK numbers private?

No. Free UK numbers come from public websites, so anyone can read the codes. For a private +44 number you need a paid provider.

Can I use a US number instead of a UK number?

For Telegram and Google, usually yes. For WhatsApp and UK services, a local +44 number is more likely to work.

Which UK number works for WhatsApp?

No free UK number passed WhatsApp in our test. SMSFAST UK numbers passed every WhatsApp check.
