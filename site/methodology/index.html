Our test protocol

Every month we test each service in our ranking from scratch. For every service we request 10 verification codes per platform from Telegram, WhatsApp and Google, using numbers from each country the service offers: the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. A request counts as a success when the code arrives within 5 minutes and can be entered.

We use fresh test accounts on real phones and browsers, and delete them after the test. Each cycle takes about two weeks.

How the score is calculated

The score out of 10 combines four criteria. OTP delivery has the biggest weight because it is what most people need.

CriterionWeightWhat we measure
OTP delivery40%Share of the 30 code requests (10 per platform) that succeeded
Privacy20%Whether the number is private to you or visible to everyone
Ease of use20%Signup steps, ads, keep-alive rules, how fast you get a number
Country coverage20%Numbers available in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia

Each criterion is rated from 0 to 10, multiplied by its weight and added up. Two services with similar delivery can end up with different scores if one shows your messages to everyone or covers only one country.

Status labels

  • Works 7 or more of 10 codes received on that platform.
  • Sometimes 3 to 6 of 10 codes received.
  • Fails 0 to 2 of 10 codes received.

What we do not do

We do not try to get around platform anti-fraud checks, we do not create accounts in bulk and we do not publish methods for doing so. We only test what an ordinary person can do with each service.
