Why it fails

Why Most Free Numbers Fail on WhatsApp

WhatsApp is the strictest of the three platforms we test. It checks the line type of every number and refuses many VoIP numbers before sending a code. Almost every free virtual number is a VoIP number, so most of them never get as far as an SMS.

WhatsApp also refuses numbers that have already been used to register many accounts, which rules out public receive-SMS numbers. And it may expect a number that matches the region of your phone and app store. A free US number on a phone set up in the UK is more likely to fail.

Hidden risk

The Hidden Risk: Someone Else Can Re-Register Your Account

A WhatsApp account is tied to its phone number. If you register with a public number, or with a free number that is later recycled, the next person to get that number can request a code and register WhatsApp on it. Your account is then moved to their phone.

To protect yourself, turn on Two-step verification in WhatsApp settings and set a PIN. Better still, use a number you control for the long term: your own SIM, or a number rented for months from a provider such as SMSFAST, rather than a one-time code.

Troubleshooting

WhatsApp Code Not Received: What to Try

  1. Wait for the timer

    WhatsApp shows a countdown before you can request another code.

  2. Choose "Call me"

    A voice call can reach some numbers that do not receive SMS.

  3. Check the country code

    Pick the right country in the list and enter the number without a leading zero.

  4. Avoid repeated requests

    Too many requests lock the number for hours or longer.

  5. Change the number type

    If a free VoIP number keeps failing, a private paid number is the realistic next step.

Business accounts

WhatsApp Business and Virtual Numbers

WhatsApp Business also needs a number that can receive an SMS or a call to register. Many VoIP numbers are not supported, so the same limits apply. If you run a business account, use a number you will keep for years, because losing it means losing access to the account and its chat history. Check WhatsApp's own help pages for the current rules before you choose a number.

FAQ

Free Virtual Number for WhatsApp: FAQ

Is there a free number that works for WhatsApp verification?

Not reliably. In our tests Google Voice passed some attempts and every other free number failed. For WhatsApp, a cheap private paid number is the realistic choice.

Why is WhatsApp not sending the code to my virtual number?

WhatsApp checks the line type and history of the number before sending a code. Most free virtual numbers are VoIP lines or heavily used, so WhatsApp refuses them without sending an SMS.

Can I verify WhatsApp with a voice call instead of SMS?

Yes. When the SMS timer ends, choose "Call me" and WhatsApp reads the 6-digit code aloud. It helps with some numbers but not with numbers WhatsApp has already refused.

Is it safe to use a public number for WhatsApp?

No. Anyone can read the code, and the next person who uses that number can re-register WhatsApp and take over your account.

Can I use a US number for WhatsApp outside the US?

Often yes, but WhatsApp may expect a number that matches your region. A local number for your country is more likely to pass.
